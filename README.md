# Retail Sales Analytics | Python, SQL & Business Insights

## Project Overview
An end-to-end Retail Sales Analytics project focused on analyzing sales performance, profitability, discount patterns, product categories, regional performance, and shipping methods.

The project uses Python for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA), and SQLite SQL for business-focused data analysis.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify profitable and loss-making product categories.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Evaluate shipping mode performance.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## Tech Stack

## Key Business Findings

1. **Technology** generated the highest total profit of 145,455.66.
2. **Furniture** recorded the lowest profit margin of 2.49%.
3. **Office Supplies** had the highest number of loss-making records (885).
4. Discount levels above 20% were associated with negative aggregate profits in this dataset.
5. The **West region** recorded the highest total sales and profit.

## Business Recommendations

- Review pricing and discount strategies for Furniture.
- Investigate loss-making transactions in Office Supplies.
- Monitor high-discount transactions to protect profitability.
- Maintain the strong performance of Technology products.
- Track regional sales and shipping performance.

## Dataset

Sample Superstore retail sales dataset containing sales, profit, quantity, discount, product category, region, and shipping information.

Note: The dataset does not contain order dates or customer identifiers, so time-based and customer-level analysis was not performed.
